# IMC Prosperity Trading Challenge 2026

This repository contains quantitative trading models, strategy implementations, and supporting research developed during the IMC Prosperity Trading Challenge hosted by IMC Trading.

Our team, **Alpha Hunters Padua**, achieved:
- 208 / 18,803 overall teams
- 184 / 18,803 in the algorithmic track
- 2 / 61 among Italian teams

The competition involved over 30,000 participants from 1,549 universities across 117 countries and focused on quantitative trading, market making, strategy design, and decision-making under uncertainty.

This repository is organized by competition rounds. Each round contains:
- strategy implementations
- supporting documentation
- round-specific instructions and analysis

The objective of this repository is to provide a structured and reproducible overview of the strategies developed during the competition. The material may also serve as a reference for future participants interested in quantitative trading and algorithmic strategy development.
